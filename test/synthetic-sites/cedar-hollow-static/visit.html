What to Expect

Services last about an hour. Dress is casual; you will see everything from jeans to suits. Greeters at the front doors can show you around, and coffee and pastries are in the lobby before each service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I park?
Our main lot is right off Quillmore Lane. Look for the six spaces marked “First-Time Guest” next to the front entrance. Overflow parking is on the gravel lot behind the Fellowship Hall.
What about my kids?
Check in at the Kids Corner desk in the children's wing (left of the lobby). Each child gets a name tag and you get a matching pickup tag. Programs run for birth through 5th grade during the 10:45 service.
Is the building accessible?
Yes. The front entrance has a ramp and automatic doors, there is an elevator to the lower level, and hearing loops are available in the sanctuary.
Can I watch online?
The 10:45 service streams live on our website every Sunday.
